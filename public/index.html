How the scores work

Each story is scored from −3 (strong headwind) to +3 (strong tailwind) for every industry and economy it touches. The map adds those scores, counting high-confidence calls at 1.0, medium at 0.7 and low at 0.4.

Industries are the largest revenue pools in the world economy. Economies are the largest by output, plus the Gulf producers that set energy prices. Stories are written without company names, so the map shows sector-level pressure only.

Scores are a structured judgement about direction and size over each story's horizon. They are not price targets or investment advice.
